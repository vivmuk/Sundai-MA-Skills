# HCP Discovery and Access

Define the scientific need and missing clinical perspectives before searching.
Use existing account/territory plans and `field-medical-planning` for capacity.
A high publication count is not the same as unmet need, accessibility or suitability.

## Discover and verify

1. Identify relevant specialty, disease focus, setting, geography and the specific
   scientific question. Include community and underserved practice perspectives
   when the task calls for them; do not equate expertise solely with seniority.
2. Search professional sources: institutional directories, relevant publications,
   trial investigator listings and published congress programs. The repository
   public-evidence gateway supports the literature and trial steps.
3. Verify each identity with at least the institution/specialty and a stable
   professional identifier where available. Mark uncertain matches for review.
4. Record name, role, institution, scientific relevance, public source/date, identity
   confidence, existing relationship and a next verification step. Do not guess email
   addresses, scrape private profiles or collect irrelevant personal information.
5. Distinguish a verified colleague/collaborator link from coauthorship inferred from
   a paper. Neither proves a personal relationship or willingness to introduce you.

## Plan access

Use published institutional procedures, an existing authorized relationship,
scientific events, a permitted professional enquiry route or a requested introduction.
Respect restrictions, contact preferences and declined contact. Do not bypass
assistants or institution access rules, conceal affiliation or manufacture urgency.

A candidate register should show: scientific need, contribution sought, evidence of
relevance, identity status, permissible access route, next action, owner and status.
Use explicit qualitative criteria rather than an invented probability of access.
Scientific potential and access feasibility are separate dimensions. Never rank by
prescribing value, inferred patient volume, sales uplift or sensitive traits.

Draft a short introduction around the scientific purpose only when useful. Sending,
calendar invitations and contact-list changes require the actual user authorization
and permitted tools. Do not call a proposed introduction a completed engagement.

In workshop mode, the linked HCPs and access log are fictional. Do not search for
these people online. Demonstrate real public discovery separately with source-backed
records, without mixing them into the fictional organization.
Read `house-rules/hcp-discovery-and-access.md`.
