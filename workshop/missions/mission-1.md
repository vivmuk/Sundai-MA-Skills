# Mission 1 — Field Medical

## 🧑‍⚕️ Prepare me for a difficult conversation

> **Your mission:** In three days you have a 30-minute scientific exchange with
> Dr Adaeze Okafor. She has been publicly sceptical about your compound and she
> knows this field better than you do. Walk in ready.

## What you have

Everything in `../data/<your TA>/`:

- `kol-dossiers.md` — background and publication record for three experts
- `interaction-notes.md` — what happened last time
- `product-profile.md` — the compound and its evidence
- `evidence-landscape.md` — what is known in this disease
- `medical-plan.md` — the current scientific priorities

Your agent can also search PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov if your network allows.

## What "done" looks like

A brief someone could actually absorb in the ten minutes before the meeting.
Not a dossier — a brief.

It should tell you who she is, what she genuinely cares about, what has changed
since you last spoke, **questions worth asking her**, the questions she is
likely to ask you, and the evidence to have ready.

## Timebox

30 minutes.

## When it finishes, push on it

> *"She thinks our data are too weak to change her practice. What would actually
> change her mind — and do we have it?"*

If the honest answer is "we don't have it", that is a finding, not a failure.
Ask the agent to say so plainly.
