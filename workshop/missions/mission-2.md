# Mission 2 — Field Insights

## 📋 Find what humans would miss

> **Your mission:** Here are a quarter's worth of field interaction records.
> Tell leadership what they need to know.

## What you have

Everything in `../data/<your TA>/`:

- `field-observations.csv` — the records themselves
- `medical-plan.md` — the current scientific priorities
- `evidence-landscape.md` — what is known
- `product-profile.md` — the compound

## What "done" looks like

Not a list of themes with counts. Themes are what a spreadsheet gives you.

You want **insights**: what is happening, why it matters, what it changes, and
what someone should do about it — with a name against each action.

Plus the three things underneath that usually get lost: where sources genuinely
**disagree**, the **weak signals** only one or two people mentioned, and what
the field conspicuously **did not** say.

## Timebox

30 minutes.

## When it finishes, push on it

> *"Which three of these should leadership care about most, and why those three
> rather than the others?"*

Then: *"What would change your mind about the first one?"*

## Watch for this

Somewhere in those records is something the agent should surface **before** it
does any analysis at all. If it does, stop and read it out to the room. If it
does not, that is worth knowing too.
