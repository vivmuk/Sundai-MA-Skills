# Mission 3 — Congress Intelligence

## 🏛️ The congress just ended. Tell leadership what changed.

> **Your mission:** Three days of data, sixty presentations, two competitor
> announcements. Leadership has four minutes. What changed, and what should we
> do about it?

## What you have

Everything in `../data/<your TA>/`:

- `congress-abstracts.md` — what was presented
- `competitor-announcements.md` — press releases and corporate statements
- `evidence-landscape.md` — what was known going in
- `medical-plan.md` — our current position and narrative
- `product-profile.md` — our compound

## What "done" looks like

Four sections, one page:

**What changed** · **Why it matters** · **What to watch** · **What we should do**

Not a summary of what was presented. A summary is what everyone else will
produce, and by the time it lands people will have formed their impressions
already.

## Timebox

30 minutes.

## When it finishes, push on it

> *"What did you expect to happen before the congress? Show me where reality
> differed."*

If the agent cannot answer that, it summarised rather than analysed — the
comparison against a prior expectation is what makes this intelligence.

Then: *"What was presented that contradicts something we currently claim?"*

## The hardest thing to get right

Apply the same scepticism to your own data as to the competitor's. If the agent
is tough on their single-arm study and generous about yours, say so — everyone
outside the company will notice that asymmetry immediately.
