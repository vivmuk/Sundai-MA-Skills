# Mission 6 — Evidence Generation

## 🔬 Tell us what we still don't know

> **Your mission:** Identify what we don't know, decide which gaps are worth
> closing, and allocate a £5 million evidence budget.

## What you have

Everything in `../data/<your TA>/`:

- `evidence-landscape.md` — what exists
- `product-profile.md` — the compound and its evidence base
- `field-observations.csv` — the questions clinicians actually ask
- `medical-plan.md` — the scientific priorities
- `publication-plan.md` — what is planned

Your agent can also check what is already running on ClinicalTrials.gov if your
network allows.

## What "done" looks like

A ranked set of gaps, each with the **decision it blocks** and **whose decision
it is**, the study design that would close it, and what it costs.

Then an allocation against the £5m — with an explicit line, and everything
below it, and what the organisation loses by not funding those.

## Timebox

30 minutes.

## The rule that makes this hard

> **Do not propose a study because data are missing. Explain why closing the gap
> changes a decision.**

Everything is unknown about everything. The list is always long and always true.
Most of it does not matter. The filter is the exercise.

## When it finishes, push on it

> *"Which of these could we answer from data we already have, without running
> anything new?"*

A surprising share of "evidence gaps" are unpublished analyses of data the
company already holds.

Then: *"Is anyone already running this? What happens if they publish first?"*

## Round 2

Your file is `house-rules/evidence-gap-analysis.md`.

Think about: what does your organisation require before it funds a study? What
makes evidence credible to the people you need to convince?
