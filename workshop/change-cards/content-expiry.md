# A content review date has passed

SYNTHETIC DATA - scenario date is 1 October 2026.

Review the content inventory for the selected therapeutic area. Assets whose review
due date has passed or whose status is draft/expired are not eligible for distribution.
Determine whether the proposed plan relies on any of them. Show the affected asset
IDs, evidence dependencies, owner role and next review action.

Prepare a corrected draft plan. Do not claim that content was withdrawn from a real
system or that a new asset was medically approved.
