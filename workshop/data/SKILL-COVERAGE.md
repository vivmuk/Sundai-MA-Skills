# Workshop synthetic-data coverage

The three therapeutic-area packs now contain the same **32-file schema**, so a
facilitator can run one job in parallel across oncology, immunology and
cardiometabolic teams and compare how the reasoning changes with the evidence.
The files are intentionally incomplete and occasionally inconsistent. They are
source material to appraise, not gold-standard answers.

## How to use the expanded packs

1. Pick a skill or a cross-functional job below.
2. Replace `<TA>` with `oncology-mm`, `immunology-ad`, or
   `cardiometabolic-obesity`.
3. Give the participant the job and named files, but not the expected traps.
4. Require an inventory of missing information before analysis and a challenge
   step before delivery.
5. Debrief against the **testable tension**, not against one prescribed answer.

Every human-sourced set includes safety or product-quality material. The
correct workshop behaviour is to surface the verbatim report before analysis,
state that the human reporting clock has already started, and route it under
the organisation's SOP. The synthetic files do not discharge that obligation.

## Cross-functional capstones

### Capstone A — label-expansion readiness

Use `launch-readiness-register.md`, `medical-information-enquiries.csv`,
`mlr-review-comments.md`, `scientific-platform-draft.md` and
`integrated-evidence-plan.csv`. Ask for a gate verdict, remediation plan,
leadership deck and one-page PDF brief.

### Capstone B — evidence investment committee

Use `iis-proposal.md`, `rwe-study-concept.md`, `payer-hta-brief.md`,
`guideline-landscape.md` and `integrated-evidence-plan.csv`. Give teams £2m and
require a portfolio choice, not five individually reasonable recommendations.

### Capstone C — source to publication

Use `patient-level-analysis.csv`, `structured-evidence-table.csv`,
`manuscript-draft.md`, `abstract-poster-brief.md`, `plain-language-source.md`
and `mlr-review-comments.md`. Require consistent denominators and claims across
the manuscript, abstract, figure and lay summary.
