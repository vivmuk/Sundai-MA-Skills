# House rules — `clinical-trials-search`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/clinical-trials-search/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Always include TERMINATED and WITHDRAWN trials in a competitive scan. The
  termination reason is often the most valuable field in the record.
- Report distinct trials, not NCT records. Our TA routinely registers regional
  cohorts separately and counting records overstates the landscape.
- Cross-check EU CTIS before stating that nothing is running in a given setting.
  ClinicalTrials.gov coverage of EU-only trials is incomplete.
