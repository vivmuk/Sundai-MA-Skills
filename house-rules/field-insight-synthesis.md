# House rules — `field-insight-synthesis`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/field-insight-synthesis/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- An observation is not an insight. An insight must explain why the observation
  matters and what decision it bears on. Move anything that stops short into the
  observations appendix rather than upgrading it.
- Always state the composition of the sample alongside any frequency. "6 of 47,
  all academic" is a different finding from "6 of 47, spread across settings".
- Do not sanitise critical or unflattering field commentary. The uncomfortable
  observations are the ones with signal in them.
