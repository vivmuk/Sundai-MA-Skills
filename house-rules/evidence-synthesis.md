# House rules — `evidence-synthesis`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/evidence-synthesis/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Separate what the evidence establishes from what our narrative asserts. If we
  cannot cite it, it is a positioning statement and must be labelled as one.
- Every synthesis ends with the gap section. Do not cut it for length — it is
  the section evidence generation actually uses.
- When trials disagree, name the candidate explanations before concluding. We do
  not average conflicting results.
