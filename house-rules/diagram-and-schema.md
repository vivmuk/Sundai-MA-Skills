# House rules — `diagram-and-schema`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/diagram-and-schema/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Treatment pathway diagrams must show where local practice diverges from the
  guideline. That gap is usually the reason the diagram is being drawn.
- PRISMA numbers must reconcile before the diagram leaves the department. Our
  reviewers do the subtraction.
- State what the diagram omits. A simplified pathway is fine; a simplified
  pathway presented as complete is not.
