# House rules — `scientific-platform`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/scientific-platform/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every core scientific statement carries its supporting evidence and the certainty of that evidence in the platform document itself.
- A statement that cannot be substantiated is recorded as an evidence gap, not as an aspiration or a 'developing' statement.
- The platform is reviewed whenever a major readout or guideline update lands, not only annually.
- Lexicon entries state what we do not say as well as what we do.
