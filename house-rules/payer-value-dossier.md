# House rules — `payer-value-dossier`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/payer-value-dossier/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- The comparator is the one used in the local treatment pathway, not the trial comparator, unless they are the same — and say which.
- Every economic input traces to a source with its uncertainty stated. Model outputs are labelled as model outputs.
- AMCP-format dossiers are unsolicited-request responses. Confirm the request is documented before releasing one.
- Indirect comparisons state their method and assumptions in the body, not in an appendix.
