# House rules — `launch-medical-readiness`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/launch-medical-readiness/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- A readiness review produces a verdict, including 'not ready'. Do not soften a gate into a plan.
- The first-six-months question list must be tested against real field input, not written from the launch narrative.
- Medical information standard response documents must exist and be approved before first promotional use, not before launch day.
- Name the owner and the date for every gap. A readiness review with unowned gaps has not finished.
