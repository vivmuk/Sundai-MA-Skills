# House rules — `medical-strategy-plan`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-strategy-plan/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every proposed activity must trace back to a stated strategic priority. Report
  the orphans by name rather than quietly dropping them.
- Every priority states what we are giving up to pursue it. A plan with no
  trade-offs has not chosen anything.
- Every success measure must be capable of coming back negative. "Number of KOL
  interactions" is an activity count, not a measure.
