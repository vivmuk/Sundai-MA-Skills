# House rules — `advisory-board-design`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/advisory-board-design/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Fair market value rates come from our approved FMV schedule — never estimate an honorarium.
- Advisor selection must be documented against written scientific criteria before any invitation goes out, and the selection rationale is retained.
- No commercial colleague attends the scientific discussion. If they attend a separate session, say so in the charter.
- Every advisory board charter names the specific decision the advice will inform. A board with no decision behind it does not get approved.
