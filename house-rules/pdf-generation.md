# House rules — `pdf-generation`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/pdf-generation/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- All externally facing documents are PDF, generated from our approved template
  at `[path]`. HTML is acceptable internally only.
- Document control footer is mandatory: document ID, version, date, review date,
  and the approving signatory.
- Never distribute a PDF with the draft marking removed unless the medical
  signatory has recorded their approval in `[system]`.
