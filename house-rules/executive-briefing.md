# House rules — `executive-briefing`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/executive-briefing/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Leadership briefings follow the `[template]` memo format: decision box top
  right, one page hard limit, appendix as a separate attachment.
- The CMO reads on paper — memos, never decks, for that audience.
- Any briefing that names a competitor readout is reviewed by `[role]`
  before distribution.
