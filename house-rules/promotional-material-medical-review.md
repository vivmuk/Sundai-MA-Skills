# House rules — `promotional-material-medical-review`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/promotional-material-medical-review/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Do not certify anything whose supporting reference you have not opened and read.
- 'Data on file' is acceptable only where the file exists, is retrievable, and you have seen it.
- Record every finding with its location, the reason, and what would make it approvable.
- If material proceeds against your advice, record your position through the escalation route. Do not simply approve.
