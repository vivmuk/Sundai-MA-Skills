# House rules — `medical-terminology-mapping`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-terminology-mapping/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Use our insight taxonomy, not MeSH, for coding field observations. The
  taxonomy is at `[link or path]` and version `[n]`.
- Never collapse fatigue and asthenia. Our clinicians distinguish them
  consistently and merging them has hidden a real signal before.
- Any aggregate built on mapped data must publish the collapsing decisions
  alongside it. A frequency count without the mapping is not interpretable.
