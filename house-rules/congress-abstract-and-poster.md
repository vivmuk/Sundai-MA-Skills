# House rules — `congress-abstract-and-poster`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/congress-abstract-and-poster/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Internal submission deadline is four weeks before the congress deadline. This
  is not negotiable; MLR cannot turn round faster.
- Confirm the poster dimensions with both the congress AND the printer in
  writing before building. We have printed the wrong size twice.
- Never submit an encore without checking the prior-publication policy.
  Withdrawal after acceptance is worse than not submitting.
