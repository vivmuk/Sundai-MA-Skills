# House rules — `medical-information-response`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-information-response/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Answer only what was asked. Scope creep turns a reactive response into a
  proactive communication, which is a regulatory problem regardless of accuracy.
- Our department does not respond to off-label enquiries in any channel. Route
  all such enquiries to `[named team]` without answering.
- Every SRD carries a review date no more than 12 months out. An out-of-date SRD
  is worse than none because it carries the authority of an approved document.
