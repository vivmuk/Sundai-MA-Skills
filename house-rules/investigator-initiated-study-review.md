# House rules — `investigator-initiated-study-review`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/investigator-initiated-study-review/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- IIS proposals are reviewed by the medical committee against written scientific criteria; commercial functions do not participate in the funding decision.
- The investigator is the sponsor and owns the protocol, the data and the publication. We do not draft the protocol.
- Support is never conditioned on prescribing, formulary status or any commercial outcome, and any suggestion of that stops the review.
- Review the pattern of funded studies annually. A portfolio that clusters around high-prescribing sites is a compliance finding regardless of the merit of each study.
