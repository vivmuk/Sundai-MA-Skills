# House rules — `strategic-analysis`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/strategic-analysis/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every proposed activity must trace back to a stated strategic priority. If it
  does not trace, either the activity goes or the priority was unstated — say
  which.
- Every recommendation states what we give up to do it. A recommendation with no
  trade-off has not been thought through.
- Do not propose guideline inclusion as a 12-month objective. Guideline cycles
  in our TA run 2-3 years and require published evidence well in advance.
