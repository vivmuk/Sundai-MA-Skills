# House rules — `congress-intelligence`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/congress-intelligence/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Readout due within 72 hours of the congress closing. A better analysis
  delivered in week three has been overtaken by everyone informal impressions.
- One page above the fold. Detail below for those who want it.
- Never cite congress abstracts in externally facing material. Internal only,
  always labelled `[abstract]`.
