# House rules — `insight-generation`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/insight-generation/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- An observation is not an insight. An insight must explain why the observation
  matters and what decision it bears on. Reject anything that stops at "KOLs
  mentioned X".
- Always report the number of independent sources AND their composition
  (academic vs community, geography). A finding from six academic centres is not
  a finding about the market.
- Weak signals from a single credible source must be reported, not filtered.
  Our most consequential missed insight in the last three years was mentioned
  once and dropped for low frequency.
