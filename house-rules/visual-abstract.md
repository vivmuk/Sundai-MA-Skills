# House rules — `visual-abstract`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/visual-abstract/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every visual abstract goes through full MLR review. They travel further than
  any other output we produce and are reviewed least carefully.
- Absolute numbers must appear alongside any relative figure. Our patient
  advocates have told us repeatedly that relative-only framing misleads.
- Check the target journal specification before designing — several prescribe
  dimensions, font sizes and panel structure.
