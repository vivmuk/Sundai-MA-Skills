# House rules — `plain-language-summary`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/plain-language-summary/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Target grade 6, not grade 8. Our patient population skews older and our
  advocates have told us repeatedly that grade 8 is too hard.
- Every summary is reviewed by at least one patient advocate before submission.
  The readability score does not substitute for this.
- Always present risk as natural frequencies out of 100, for both arms, with the
  same denominator throughout.
