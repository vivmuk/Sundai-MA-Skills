# House rules — `medical-education-program`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-education-program/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Independent education grants are decided by the grant committee with no commercial participation, against published criteria, and every decision and rationale is recorded — including declines.
- We do not suggest content, faculty, title or format for an independently funded programme, and we do not review its materials.
- Speaker programme content is medically approved and speaker selection is documented against written criteria. Sales does not select speakers.
- Every programme states its evidenced educational need and the barrier it addresses. If the barrier is access or cost, the programme does not proceed as education.
