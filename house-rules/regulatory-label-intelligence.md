# House rules — `regulatory-label-intelligence`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/regulatory-label-intelligence/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Use the EU SmPC, not the US label, as our default reference. Our primary
  markets are European and the indications differ.
- Never put FAERS counts in an externally facing document. Internal
  hypothesis-generation only, and never comparatively against a competitor.
- Any deliverable discussing efficacy carries the boxed warning content in the
  same section, not in an appendix.
