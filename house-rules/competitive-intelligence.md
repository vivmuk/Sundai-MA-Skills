# House rules — `competitive-intelligence`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/competitive-intelligence/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Competitor information comes only from public sources — publications, congress materials, registries, regulatory documents, company disclosures. Record the source for every assertion.
- Never solicit non-public information from a competitor's employee, investigator or vendor, and stop and report if it is offered.
- Competitive assessments are internal. They are not the basis for any external comparative statement without head-to-head evidence.
- Apply the same appraisal standard to a competitor's data as to our own; the analysis is read by people who will notice if we do not.
