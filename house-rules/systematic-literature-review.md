# House rules — `systematic-literature-review`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/systematic-literature-review/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Our HTA submissions require Embase and CENTRAL in addition to PubMed. A
  PubMed-only search cannot be described as systematic in any document leaving
  the department.
- Register the protocol in PROSPERO before searching. Retrospective registration
  is treated as no registration by our submission targets.
- Always list full-text exclusions with reasons. This is the first thing our
  reviewers check.
