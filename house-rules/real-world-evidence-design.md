# House rules — `real-world-evidence-design`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/real-world-evidence-design/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- The protocol is registered before analysis begins, and the registration number appears in every output.
- New-user, active-comparator is the default. Any other design requires written justification from epidemiology.
- The outcome algorithm must be validated in the specific data source, with its PPV stated. An unvalidated outcome is a feasibility question, not a study.
- Report an E-value or equivalent for every comparative estimate.
