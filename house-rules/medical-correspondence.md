# House rules — `medical-correspondence`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-correspondence/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- DHCP letters require regulatory sign-off and, in most markets, agreement with
  the regulator before distribution. Never send one on the strength of a draft.
- Use the approved letterhead at `[path]`. Letters on any other template are
  returned.
- Every letter discussing a product carries adverse event reporting instructions
  with the actual local reporting route, not a generic statement.
