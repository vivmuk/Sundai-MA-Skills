# House rules — `guideline-engagement`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/guideline-engagement/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- No contact with a guideline committee member about a guideline position. Scientific exchange about the evidence is fine; advocacy for an outcome is not.
- Guideline timelines in any plan are worked backwards from the published evidence cut-off, not the publication date.
- Responses to public consultations go through medical and legal review and are submitted on the record.
- A near-term guideline inclusion objective must state the published evidence that would support it. If there is none, say so rather than listing engagement activities.
