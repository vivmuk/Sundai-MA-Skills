# House rules — `scientific-manuscript`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/scientific-manuscript/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Our publication steering committee approves the target journal before drafting
  begins. Do not draft to a journal that has not been agreed.
- Medical writing support is always disclosed by name, agency and funder. No
  exceptions, including for internal writers.
- Complete the EQUATOR checklist as you draft and submit it with the manuscript.
  Retrofitting it after drafting is how items get missed.
