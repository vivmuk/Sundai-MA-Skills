# House rules — `scientific-communication-strategy`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/scientific-communication-strategy/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Separate an evidence gap from a communication gap explicitly for every
  priority. Proposing a publication to fix an evidence gap wastes a year.
- Negative and neutral results are published. If the plan contains none, state
  whether there are any and why they are not scheduled.
- Do not list guideline inclusion as a 12-month objective. Committee cycles in
  our TA run 2-3 years and the evidence must be published well in advance.
