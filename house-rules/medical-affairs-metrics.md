# House rules — `medical-affairs-metrics`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-affairs-metrics/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every metric must be capable of coming back negative, and must name the decision a bad result would trigger.
- Activity and impact measures are reported separately. A scorecard may not present activity volume as impact.
- No metric may be correlated with prescribing at the individual HCP level, and no promotional constructs — share of voice, message recall, message penetration.
- State the attribution level — direct, contributory or associated — for every impact claim.
