# House rules — `kol-engagement-brief`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/kol-engagement-brief/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Two pages maximum, hard limit. Our MSLs read these in the car park and a
  longer brief is simply not read.
- Never include prescribing data, influence tiers, or commercial segmentation.
  Their presence turns a medical document into evidence that Medical was
  operating commercially.
- Every brief includes at least one question we genuinely do not know the answer
  to. If we know what they will say, the meeting is not scientific exchange.
