# House rules — `integrated-evidence-plan`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/integrated-evidence-plan/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Every evidence item names the decision it serves, the audience, an owner, a date and a publication route.
- The HTA comparator is assessed at plan time. If it differs from the trial comparator, that is recorded as a risk with a mitigation.
- Investigator-initiated studies are never counted as planned company evidence deliverables.
- The plan states what falls below the funding line and what the organisation gives up by not doing it.
